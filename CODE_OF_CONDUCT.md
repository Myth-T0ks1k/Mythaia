# Code of conduct

Mythaia is used by people in many countries, often in difficult circumstances. Everyone who takes
part in this project — in issues, pull requests, discussions or reviews — is expected to make that
participation welcoming, patient and respectful.

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be kind and assume good intent; many contributors write in a second language.
- Critique code and ideas, never people.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory language or sharing of anyone's private information.
- Never ask anyone to reveal where they are or which network they use.

Problems can be reported privately to the maintainers through the repository's
[Security tab](https://github.com/Myth-T0ks1k/Mythaia/security/advisories/new) or to any
maintainer on GitHub. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and block participants who do
not follow these rules.
